Privacy - टंकण

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Your practice progress stays on your device

Your lessons, speeds, accuracy, unlocked keys, settings and book positions are saved in your browser on your device. They stay there unless you choose account saving or public leaderboard participation as described below. Signing in or out never deletes your local progress. Clearing browser data erases the local copy; use Settings → Export progress to make a backup.

If you choose to sign in with Google

Sign-in is optional and handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service). When you sign in, Google shares your basic Google profile - your name, email address and profile photo - with this site so it can show who is signed in. That sign-in record is stored by Google in the site's Firebase project, and it stays there after you sign out, until it is deleted (see below).

While you are signed in, Firebase stores your sign-in session in this browser's storage (IndexedDB/localStorage, under Firebase's own entries) so you stay signed in between visits. Signing out clears that session from this browser.

If you choose to save your progress to your account (the site asks first), a copy of your practice progress - lessons, speeds, unlocked keys and settings - is stored in the site's Firebase project under your account, so it survives a lost or changed device and can be restored when you sign in elsewhere. It updates as you practice, it is readable only by your signed-in account, and it is never public. Signing out stops further saving; the account copy remains until you ask for it to be deleted (below). Progress on a device where you never sign in still stays only in that browser, as described above.

The site does not request your location or contacts. There is no advertising today; this page will be updated before advertising is added. Nothing is sold to anyone. In addition to optional Google sign-in, account saving and leaderboard sharing, your browser requests the site's typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Google sees your IP address and browser details for those requests; the site does not send it your private practice history.

The site is hosted by Cloudflare: its servers deliver the pages to your browser, which means your browser connects to Cloudflare to load the site. Cloudflare also loads its own Web Analytics beacon (a script from static.cloudflareinsights.com) into the pages it serves; Cloudflare describes this beacon as cookieless page-visit counting. That measurement is Cloudflare's, under Cloudflare's own terms - the site itself runs no analytics or tracking scripts of its own.

Signing out and removing your sign-in record

You can sign out at any time from Profile → Account; this ends the session in your browser. To have your sign-in record (name, email, profile photo) removed from the site's Firebase project entirely, email tankanapp@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

Optional community leaderboard

Signing in does not publish your Google identity, progress or results. To take part, enter and save a public display name, then separately confirm the public-profile checkbox and choose Join leaderboard in Profile. The display name is not automatically copied from Google. Duplicate names are allowed. Names cannot contain email addresses, phone numbers, links or contact handles.

The public leaderboard shows the chosen display name and one best qualifying lesson score per person, up to 20 entries. A lesson needs at least 50 correct Remington input units and 10 distinct input keys. Results expire from the public ranking after 14 days. Clicking a name shows only that participant's qualifying score, speed, accuracy and count of qualifying shared lessons from the last 14 days. There is no messaging or contact feature. Google names, email addresses, photos, private learning history and Google account identifiers are never included in public responses.

After you join, new completed lesson summaries can be sent to a separate Cloudflare D1 database. Summaries include a private account identifier, a random lesson identifier, completion time, score, speed, accuracy, correct-input count and key-variety count. A random public profile identifier is separate from your Google account identifier. Summary data supports ranking and possible review of unusual results. Results are reported by browsers; checking a formula does not prove that a human typed the lesson. No automatic speed-based ban is used.

Use Share saved results from this device to explicitly submit eligible saved summaries from the last 14 days. Old results without key-variety metadata stay in your personal history and cannot be qualified retrospectively. Leaving the leaderboard hides your profile and scores from public reads and stops future sharing. It does not delete your private saved progress or sign you out. Private summaries may remain until cleanup; email the address below to request removal of your community record.
