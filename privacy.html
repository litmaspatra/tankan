Privacy - टंकण

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Your practice progress stays on your device

Everything you type on टंकण - lessons, speeds, accuracy, unlocked keys, settings, book positions - is stored only in your own browser's storage on your own device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Signing in or out never changes, uploads, or deletes it. Clearing your browser data erases it; use Settings → Export progress to move it to another device.

If you choose to sign in with Google

Sign-in is optional and handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service). When you sign in, Google shares your basic Google profile - your name, email address and profile photo - with this site so it can show who is signed in. That sign-in record is stored by Google in the site's Firebase project, and it stays there after you sign out, until it is deleted (see below).

While you are signed in, Firebase stores your sign-in session in this browser's storage (IndexedDB/localStorage, under Firebase's own entries) so you stay signed in between visits. Signing out clears that session from this browser. This is separate from your practice progress, which sign-in never reads or writes.

That is all the site itself collects today. It does not collect your scores, typing data, location, or contacts, and there is no advertising on the site. Nothing is sold to anyone. The only two ways information leaves your browser are the Google sign-in described above and the hosting provider's role described below.

The site is hosted by Cloudflare: its servers deliver the pages to your browser, which means your browser connects to Cloudflare to load the site. Cloudflare also loads its own Web Analytics beacon (a script from static.cloudflareinsights.com) into the pages it serves; Cloudflare describes this beacon as cookieless page-visit counting. That measurement is Cloudflare's, under Cloudflare's own terms - the site itself runs no analytics or tracking scripts of its own.

Signing out and removing your sign-in record

You can sign out at any time from Settings → Account; this ends the session in your browser. To have your sign-in record (name, email, profile photo) removed from the site's Firebase project entirely, email tankanapp@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

Future features

If features like score syncing or leaderboards are added later, this page will be updated to describe them before they launch, and anything new that leaves your device will be described here first.
