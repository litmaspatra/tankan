Privacy - Tankan (टंकण)

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Your practice progress stays on your device

Everything you type on Tankan - lessons, speeds, accuracy, unlocked keys, settings, book positions - is stored only in your own browser's storage on your own device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Signing in or out never changes, uploads, or deletes it. Clearing your browser data erases it; use Settings → Export progress to move it to another device.

If you choose to sign in with Google

Sign-in is optional and handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service). When you sign in, Google shares your basic Google profile - your name, email address and profile photo - with this site so it can show who is signed in. That sign-in record is stored by Google in the site's Firebase project. Firebase keeps your signed-in session in your browser's storage so you stay signed in between visits.

That is all the site collects today. It does not collect your scores, typing data, location, or contacts. There is no advertising on the site and no analytics tracking. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties beyond the Google sign-in service described above.

Signing out and removing your sign-in record

You can sign out at any time from Settings → Account; this ends the session in your browser. To have your sign-in record (name, email, profile photo) removed from the site's Firebase project entirely, email tankanapp@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

Future features

If features like score syncing or leaderboards are added later, this page will be updated to describe them before they launch, and anything new that leaves your device will be described here first.
